Each of the 118 new candidates was then resolved against **two or more pages** (official site where it exists, plus at least one listing/aggregator and one review source) before any row was written. Anything that could not be resolved to a single real location, or whose sources disagreed on a material fact, was either rejected with a written reason or added with `open_on_trip_date = null` plus a flag.
