## How pass 9 searched (Cupertino first, then the ring)

1. `yelp.com/search` "cheap lunch specials / lunch special(s)" pages for each city, September 2025-2026
   snapshots, were mined for the *tagged* attribute "Lunch specials" plus the attached review quote.
2. Every candidate then needed a **locatable** address + weekly hours before it could join the master list -
   taken from Yelp business-page blocks, the business's own site, TripAdvisor/Autoreserve/Apple Maps/corner.inc
   JSON blocks, or menu directories (restaurantji, zmenu, allmenus, wa-cafe, kwickmenu). Rows that could not be
   pinned were put in the rejects file with the link that shows why, never padded with guesses.
3. Prices print **only** where a restaurant's own menu page shows them (this pass: Mandarin Gourmet's own site,
   The Diner of Los Gatos' own menu page, IHOP Milpitas' own specials page, plus happy-lamb-style business-written
   Yelp update blocks recorded at review level). Reviewer and aggregator figures (e.g. "under $10", "$14.99 trio
   roll", "$9.99/$14.99 Early Dine", the $8.99 EPL lunch-box mirrors) stay in flags as leads.
4. Two business traps were caught and documented: Saratoga **Springs NY** "lunch specials" (excluded - see
   LUNCH-FLAG-24) and La Vigna listings that resolve to Queens, NY. Four closure banners were honoured:
   Pot Sticker King, Su Zhe Eatery, La Strada (Palo Alto), China Delight (Palo Alto).
