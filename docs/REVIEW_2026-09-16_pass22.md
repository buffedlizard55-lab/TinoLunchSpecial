## Limitations (what blocks a bigger yield)

- **The fetch proxy outage cost this pass ~30 minutes of verification capacity.** With
  fetches working, the same 45 queries would have promoted Espetus and Central Park Bistro
  to official level and confirmed or killed the 12 leads.
- **Ring saturation is real, not lazy.** After 22 passes the Cupertino core (114 rows) and
  the dense ring cities (Sunnyvale 93, San Jose 87, Palo Alto 84, Santa Clara 84, Mountain
  View 62, Fremont 58) contain essentially every walk-in lunch special that publishes a
  price. The remaining candidates are (a) chains without lunch programs, (b) fine dining
  without lunch, (c) closures, or (d) same-name traps in other states. Finding the next 100
  honest rows requires either a wider radius (Peninsula cities, Gilroy/Morgan Hill) or
  seasonal specials (restaurant weeks) that the project deliberately excludes.
- **Aggregator-only evidence stays at 'listing' level** by policy, so several real specials
  (Shabuway's old $13.99 lunch, B Street & Vine's lunch menu) can never reach 'official'
  without a working direct fetch of the restaurant's own site.
- **The trip-day fit was never the point of these four rows** - all are 14-18 miles from
  the Apple Park destination; the list documents the market, the transit page plans the trip.
