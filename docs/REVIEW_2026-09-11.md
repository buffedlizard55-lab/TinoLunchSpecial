## What was wrong and got fixed

**The front-page summary sentence was printing garbage.** `assets/app.js` interpolates
`search_protocol.candidates_found` and `search_protocol.queries_run` straight into prose
(`"selected from " + candidates_found + " candidates checked line by line across " + queries_run + " queries"`),
and into `stat()` on the Method tab, which expects a number. Both fields held whole sentences, so the live
page read:

> 523 entries in the master list, selected from **all passes documented plus ~100 new ninth-pass candidates
> (341 dedupe hits against the master list were also checked and dropped)** candidates checked line by line
> across **cumulative ~385 searches across nine passes (~90 in the ninth)** queries on 2026-09-07 through
> 2026-09-10.

That was visible on the deployed site. Fixed by making the two fields integer counts (400 / 553), moving the
prose into `queries_run_note` / `candidates_found_note`, printing the candidate note as its own line, and
adding two guards so it cannot come back:

| Guard | Where | Proven to fire? |
|---|---|---|
| `search_protocol.{queries_run,candidates_found,added_to_master,rejected_or_deferred}` must be `int`, and the two totals must match the files they summarise | `scripts/build_data.py` | Yes - injecting the old prose made the validator exit 1 with `ERRORS` |
| The rendered lead must actually print its own counts, and the two fields must be numbers | `scripts/smoke_test.js` | Yes - injecting the old prose into `data/generated.js` made the smoke test exit 1 with `!! search_protocol.queries_run is string ...` |

Both were tested by injecting the old broken value, observing the failure, then restoring.

## The 100-new-entries target was not met, and why

The task asked for 100 new entries. **This pass added 10.** The shortfall is recorded rather than papered
over, because the binding constraint was verification, not search volume:

1. **Yelp is no longer reachable as a bulk source.** A direct fetch of
   `https://www.yelp.com/search?find_desc=Lunch+Specials&find_loc=Cupertino%2C+CA` returned **HTTP 403**.
   Pass 9 mined exactly those pages for its 68 rows. Yelp facts are still usable when search surfaces them,
   but a page cannot be opened to confirm hours, so nothing resting on Yelp alone is levelled `official`
   (LUNCH-FLAG-32).
2. **The master list is now 533 rows / 513 distinct restaurant names.** Of ~30 candidates screened this pass, every
   obvious Cupertino, Sunnyvale, Santa Clara, Mountain View, Campbell and Los Gatos name already resolved to
   an existing row - the merge pipeline reported 424 dedupe hits across the replayed batches.
3. **The address-and-hours bar was held.** `scripts/build_data.py` errors on any row whose address is a
   placeholder, so a candidate needs a citable street address *and* weekly hours before it can be merged.
   Urban Plates, Gurkha Kitchen, RBG Bar & Grill and Teriyaki Madness all had verified lunch prices from the
   chamber page and were still **deferred**, because the page links only to brand homepages - no locatable
   Sunnyvale address. Merging them on a guessed address would have been exactly the hallucination the task
   forbids.
4. **Two tool calls were wasted on my own error.** I passed two malformed proxy URLs instead of the canonical
   page URLs; both returned `SignatureDoesNotMatch`. `scripts/check_links.py` already bans `routify`/`oss-`/
   `proxy` hosts for this reason. Recorded in `data/sources.json` `dead_ends`.

Reaching 100 genuinely-verified new rows needs a bulk source that publishes address + weekly hours + lunch
price together. Two were found and used this pass and both are still under-exploited:

* `https://www.svcoc.org/sunnyvale-restaurant-week-where-to-dine/` - per-restaurant lunch prices for ~13
  Sunnyvale venues (9 of them not yet in the master list).
* TripAdvisor city pages emit JSON-LD with `streetAddress`, `geo` and per-day `openingHoursSpecification` for
  several venues per page - one fetch resolved Opa! Campbell's address, hours and coordinates at once.
