## Why the net-new count is 8 and not 100

The honest arithmetic, stated up front. The master list arrived at this pass with 850 rows already covering Cupertino, Sunnyvale, Santa Clara, San Jose, Mountain View, Palo Alto, Los Altos, Campbell, Los Gatos, Saratoga, Milpitas, Menlo Park, Fremont, Newark, Union City, Redwood City, San Mateo, San Carlos, Foster City and Portola Valley's neighbours. Before a single verification search was spent, every candidate name was grepped against a **1,031-key `norm(name)|norm(city)` dedupe index** built from the 850 master rows plus the 183 reject rows.

Roughly 100 names were screened. The overwhelming majority came back as dedupe hits — which is the expected and correct outcome after sixteen prior passes. The dense core of the 10-15 mile ring is close to exhausted at the level of "restaurants that publish something citable about lunch."

**Nothing was padded to close the gap.** A padded row would be a duplicate, and a duplicate is a hallucination in table form. Eight rows survived with citable evidence; nine further findings were worth recording as rejections or bad-source notes; the rest were logged as cross-checks inside the batch file. LUNCH-FLAG-104 records the shortfall explicitly.

### Row-by-row, what was actually read

**L851 Birk's Restaurant** — 3955 Freedom Circle, Santa Clara. The restaurant's own site prints `Lunch Mon-Fri: 11am - 2:30pm, Dinner Daily from 5pm`. Its menu page links a downloadable lunch card, `Lunch_SP2026.pdf`, which was fetched and read line by line: Organic Mixed Greens 14, Caesar / Chop House / Baby Spinach 16, Wedge 18, clam chowder cup 9 / bowl 11, Flatbread 20, Prime House-Ground Beef Burger & Fries 22, Impossible Burger 24, Ahi Tuna Sandwich 26, Lobster-Crab Roll 26, Cheesesteak Sandwich 27, and the Birk's Executive entree salad priced by protein (chicken 24 → filet mignon 34). Flagged on the row: this is **not a fixed-price special** — the same PDF also carries $60-$96 steaks, so the $14-$27 band is the lunch-only portion of a lunch-only card. Third-party mirrors (Yelp, allmenus, Uber Eats) carry an older price set; the restaurant's own April 2026 PDF is what is recorded.

**L852 Barbayani Greek Taverna** — 388 Main Street, Los Altos. Own website: `LUNCH Daily: 11:30 - 2:30 pm / DINNER Daily: 5 - 9 pm, Fri & Sat: 5 - 9:30`. The restaurant's own Toast ordering page carries an explicitly separate **"Barbayani Lunch Menu"** section, and several dishes are genuinely cheaper there than on the adjacent dinner card (Moschari Souvlaki $33.00 lunch vs $34.00 dinner; Mousakka $28.00 vs $31.50). Kotopoulo Gyro $23.00 is the entry point. This is an a-la-carte lunch menu, not a discounted prix fixe — recorded as such.

**L853 The Courtyard Long Bar & Bistro** — 4320 El Camino Real Ste 101, Los Altos. The clearest qualifying special found this pass: the restaurant's own menu page prints **"Soup & Sandwich Special $17 — A Toasted Grilled Cheese with a Cup of Rich Tomato Basil Soup with House-Made Potato Chips."** The site's own structured data names each service window (Lunch Mon-Sat 11:00-13:30, Sunday Brunch 10:00-14:00). The lunch card bottoms out at a $7 hot dog and $10 grilled cheese. **Lunch closes at 1:30 PM** — the earliest cut-off on this list, and a reviewer independently notes it.

**L854 Sorelle Italian Bistro** — 915 S San Tomas Aquino Rd, Campbell. Listed for a verified *window* with **deliberately empty price columns**. The site states its lunch hours twice in identical terms (`Lunch: Wed Thurs and Friday 11:30-2:00`, `Closed Monday!!!`) but links only dinner, dessert and catering menus. No lunch price exists on any page the restaurant controls, so none was printed.

**L855 Senza Italian Kitchen** — 10 Victory Ln, Los Gatos. Formerly The Polenteria; OpenTable still routes under the old slug. Marked **conflicting** on hours: OpenTable and AAA both render the lunch block as "Wed-Fri 11:00 am-2:00 **am**", an obvious typo, while AAA's own JSON-LD drops the lunch block entirely. The restaurant's own Instagram (`Lunch: Wed - Fri 11AM - 2PM`) and Yelp agree with each other and are what is recorded. Its own Toast menu has only Dinner and Brunch tabs — no lunch pricing. A reviewer's "$40 verbal special" is held as a quote in the row flag, not as a price.

**L856 Hibari** — 3130 Alpine Road #240, Portola Valley. A brand-new kappo room from ex-Nobu Palo Alto chef Takashi Sega; its own Yelp description names the lunch format ("one soup, three dishes"). Price columns empty **because the restaurant publishes its menu solely as a Canva image slideshow with no readable text**. Press figures (Mercury News: lunch sets $18-$66; Palo Alto Online: Yurinchi set $18, dashimaki tamago set $28) are quoted in the row flags only. `open_on_trip_date` is set **false** — see LUNCH-FLAG-108.

**L857 Crepevine Palo Alto** — 367 University Avenue. An **absence result, recorded deliberately**: one all-day menu, no lunch special, no midday discount, no time-limited pricing. It earns a row because the master list already carries the Mountain View Crepevine and this is a separate address with its own hours and its own price card — and because chain location pages price differently store to store, so the Mountain View row's figures must not be reused here.

**L858 The Good Salad Campbell** — 397 E Campbell Ave. Another honest absence: no lunch special exists. Kept because the prices are flat, readable on the company's own menu, and cheap by local standards ($15 Blazing Bird, $16 Steak Supreme, $16 Samurai Steak), which makes it a usable midday option even without a discount. Hours conflict noted: a chamber directory says 11am open, the company's own page / Yelp / Apple Maps all say 10am — the company's own figure is recorded.

## Verification discipline applied this pass

1. Every candidate was **deduped before it was researched** — the pass-16 lesson, applied from the first name.
2. A price appears in `price_from` / `price_to` **only if it was read on the restaurant's own site or its own ordering menu.** Press figures (Hibari $18-$66), reviewer figures (Senza $40), photo captions (Tal Palo $15) and delivery-platform cards (Olivia Bros combos) are quoted inside flags and never in price columns.
3. **Absence is a legitimate result.** "No lunch special published" (Crepevine, The Good Salad) and "closed for lunch on Tuesdays" (Sorelle, Senza) are recorded as findings, not gaps.
4. **Event pricing is not a standing special** — the Saratoga NY Restaurant Week page was rejected on that ground as well as on geography.
5. Every row carries clickable official and review links for manual review, and every rejection carries the page that produced the name.
