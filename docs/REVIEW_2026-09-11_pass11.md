# QA record - eleventh lunch pass (2026-09-11)

Scope per the task: leave the route-planning system alone, keep building the lunch-special list for
Cupertino and the 10-15 mile ring, keep the deals at the top of the site, keep the transit plan on its own
subpage. **No transit data file (`plan.json`, `transit_outbound.json`, `transit_return.json`, `fares.json`)
was edited this pass.** `scripts/check_links.py` was edited (a false positive in its proxy-host rule, see
LUNCH-FLAG-35) - it is a lunch-side validator, and the change only removes a wrong match.

## How this pass was run

1. **Dedupe before search.** Before opening a single page, 115 candidate `name|city` pairs were run through a
   script that compares them against both `data/lunch_specials.json` and `data/lunch_rejected.json`: **85 new,
   30 already listed**. That saved the search budget for candidates that could actually become rows, and it is
   the reason the rejects file now carries 30 fewer near-misses than it would have.
2. **Cupertino first, then the ring.** Milpitas (12 rows), Fremont / Newark (8), Santa Clara and the San Jose
   Korean row (14), Los Gatos / Los Altos / Palo Alto (8), the Cupertino-Sunnyvale core (12), San Jose cheap
   eats (8), Santana Row and the chain row (10).
3. **A final sweep for published prices.** Eight city sweeps (`"lunch special" + <street>`) aimed at
   restaurants that actually print a lunch number, which is where Boda ($5.99), Home Eat ($12.99/$13.99),
   Lotus Thai and Tatami Buffet came from.
4. **Merge, then audit.** 75 candidates were written to `data/incoming/batch11a..11h`. The merge added 61 rows.
   An address re-check immediately afterwards found two of them to be duplicates and they were withdrawn the
   same day (LUNCH-FLAG-35). **Net: 59 new rows, L534-L592.**

## The 100-new-entries target was not met, and why

The task asks for 100 new entries. **This pass added 59.** Recorded rather than padded:

1. **The ring is nearly exhausted for name-level discovery.** Of 115 dedupe-checked candidates, 30 were
   already in the list. Of the 75 candidates written to `data/incoming/`, a further **14** turned out, on
   merge, to be restaurants already listed under a slightly different name - Home Eat Santa Clara, Tai Pan
   Dim Sum (Tai Pan BBQ Express) Fremont, Cam Hung, Tofu Plus, Kabab & Curry's, Tamarine, Ramen Nagi, Pho
   Nguyen, Desi Dhaba, Sumiya Yakitori, The Sandwich Maker, bb.q Chicken Sunnyvale, Asia Village and Masa
   Sushi Japan. The address de-duplication caught all 14 and printed `address already listed` rather than
   creating a second row.
2. **Yelp still returns HTTP 403 to a direct fetch** (LUNCH-FLAG-32), which remains the single biggest loss
   of bulk discovery. Everything Yelp-sourced this pass came from search snippets and is levelled
   `listing` or `conflicting`, never `official`.
3. **Closures ate a whole sweep.** Four of the fourteen Santana Row / Castro Street candidates - Left Bank
   Brasserie, Rosie McCann's, Yankee Pier and Vida Tapas Y Cocteles - are closed, and Sushi Confidential's
   downtown room has no Tuesday service. That is five candidates, roughly a batch and a half, that produced
   reject rows instead of master rows.
4. **No price was invented to fill a column.** Eight cheap-lunch figures surfaced on Reddit or third-party
   "specials" sites (LUNCH-FLAG-39). Every one is quoted inside its row and every one leaves `price_from` /
   `price_to` empty. Only **5** of the 59 new rows carry a price a business actually published (L534 Wagyu Grill AYCE $25.95, L535 Annapoorna $12.00-$23.00, L546 Dosa Express $8.45, L553 Chungdam $60.00-$99.00, L569 Campbell Cafe &amp; More $13.95-$15.95).
5. **Boda's $5.99 lunch cannot be linked.** Its own site serves http only (HTTP 500 over https), so under the
   project's https-only citation rule the price is described in the row and flagged, not printed
   (LUNCH-FLAG-37). It is the cheapest verified lunch in eleven passes and it is still one phone call away
   from being bookable.
