## Limitations (what blocks a bigger yield)

- **Ring saturation is real.** After 23 passes the dense core cities hold essentially every
  walk-in lunch special that publishes a price; the remaining candidates are chains without
  lunch programs, fine dining without lunch, closures, or same-name traps in other states.
- **JS-only menus** (Toast, chowbus, DoorDash sections) cannot be read by the text fetcher;
  three rows are held at listing level because of it.
- **Prices printed only as menu images** (Shiki Bistro) can never fill the price columns
  without OCR.
- **Aggregator-only evidence stays below official level by policy**, so delivery-menu prices
  (Rajbhog Thali's $21.99) are always labeled as such.
- **The merge script's lack of withdrawal memory** means any future row removal must also
  clean the originating incoming file (done for all 8 this pass).
