## Irregularities and decisions

- Namaste's menu labels the items “Lunch Special Thali,” but does not attach days to each item. The restaurant-controlled location page proves a lunch window every day. A disclosed 3% credit-card surcharge remains in the row flags.
- ROOH has a separate weekday lunch menu, but this is not represented as a discount relative to dinner. The price range is only the range printed in that lunch section.
- Complete ROOH weekly hours are listing-sourced; the first-party Toast page provides the restaurant identity, address, lunch section and prices.
- No rating number was copied into the dataset because ratings change continuously. Every row has direct Yelp and Google Maps review links for a current manual check.

## Site correction

The front page now defaults its full table to **Actual lunch specials only**. The existing master deliberately contains negative research rows (“no lunch special”) to prevent repeated searching; previously those ordinary all-day priced menus could crowd the visible list and even enter the top-deals strip. A single shared predicate now excludes them from deal-first views. Users can uncheck the filter to inspect the complete research ledger.

The public-transport route planner and all transit data were left untouched.
