## Flags

- **FLAG-1** (important) The N Judah does not serve Caltrain's 22nd Street station - Plans use the SF terminal for boarding and alighting and still publish the 22nd Street times in the same rows for reference. Both are Zone 1, so the fare is unchanged, and every weekday southbound train you board at the terminal also stops at 22nd St 4-5 minutes later (5:00/5:35/6:24/6:30/6:53/7:00/7:24/7:30/7:53/8:00/8:24/8:30 AM).
- **FLAG-2** (watch) SFMTA service change in effect since Saturday Aug 29 2026 - Times were read from the live SFMTA timetable pages for the date 20260908, which matched the general weekday table exactly.
- **FLAG-3** (data conflict) SFMTA's own N Bus headway text contradicts its N Bus timetable - The timetable was treated as authoritative because it is the document riders are pointed to for times, and the plan is built around the 5:15 a.m. trip. If SFMTA is really running hourly in that window, the next bus is 5:43 a.m. and the 6:20 a.m. Caltrain is still made (arrives 6:21 a.m.).
- **FLAG-4** (note) The two earliest Caltrain southbounds are unreachable from the N Judah corridor - Plan A is built on the 6:20 a.m. express; the 6:25 a.m. local is Plan A2.
- **FLAG-5** (verification gap) Caltrain minute-level times were read from the live station tables, not the print PDF - Only 1XX Local / 4XX Limited / 5XX Express / 8XX South County Connector columns were used (6XX are weekend-only, and 9/8/2026 is a normal weekday). Every row is labelled 'official-live' and train numbers are described as 'the grid column they appear in' rather than asserted. Please re-check the two Caltrain times per direction against the PDF or trip planner before you travel.
- **FLAG-6** (risk) 10-minute Caltrain-to-VTA connection on the fastest outbound plan - Plan A2 keeps the same Muni trip but takes the 6:25 a.m. local for a 12-minute-plus margin and a later bus (7:40 a.m.), arriving about 28 minutes later.
- **FLAG-7** (note) Two Cupertino-side stops are not published timepoints - Marked 'interpolated' in every affected row, with the neighbouring published times shown in the note. Treat as plus or minus 3 minutes.
- **FLAG-8** (note) Walk legs are estimates from OpenStreetMap, not agency data - Labelled 'estimate'. Re-time them once on foot if you want the totals exact; none of them changes a connection except in the FLAG-6 case.
- **FLAG-9** (note) Route 55 has a peak-only variant and one school-dependent trip - Return options name the alignment they were read from, and no plan uses the 7:26 a.m. school trip.
- **LUNCH-FLAG-1** (important) Third-party menu prices are not the dine-in price - Aggregator prices are never used as the verified price in the master list. Rows that depend on an aggregator are marked 'needs review'.
- **LUNCH-FLAG-2** (important) A lunch special from another city's branch leaked into search results - Excluded. The Cupertino row records only what the Cupertino sources show and is flagged 'lunch special not confirmed for the Cupertino location'.
